Head-coupled perspective

The screen becomes a window into a room behind it. Your head position, from metric mode, moves the viewpoint, so the room shifts as you move your head and keeps its depth. Works best with one eye closed. Calibrate the screen size with a bank card from the panel, and check it with Calibration check.
